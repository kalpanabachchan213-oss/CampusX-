💡 Know About Us

What is CampusX?

A Digital Platform for Students

CampusX is a student learning and campus management portal created to provide useful academic and campus information in one organized platform.

The platform connects students with study resources, college events, projects and learning opportunities.

Our goal is to create a simple, organized and student-friendly digital environment where students can learn, create and grow.
